What's happening down there?

This is a tiny biological simulation, not a video to watch passively — it keeps running until you reload the page.

  • The queen lays an egg whenever there's enough stored food and room for another ant.
  • Eggs become larvae, then pupae, then hatch into a brand new worker — forager, nurse, or digger.
  • Nurses tend the nursery and ferry food to the growing larvae.
  • Foragers climb to the surface, wander for seeds and leaves, and lay a scent trail home when they find one — so the colony gets better at finding food over time.
  • Diggers keep extending the tunnels deeper and wider as the colony grows.

Every load (and every "New Colony") digs a completely different hive. Click the sky to drop food, click the dirt to greet an ant, or use F / M / R / ? on your keyboard.
