# A ant colony with real hive 

This is a living, side-view ant colony simulation rendered entirely in hand-drawn crayon style. A queen lays eggs that grow into larvae and pupae, nurses tend the nursery, forager ants wander the surface hunting for food and lay scent trails home, and digger ants keep tunnelling the colony deeper — all procedurally generated fresh on every load or reset. Visitors can click the sky to drop food or click the dirt to greet an ant, with a minimal HUD showing live population, stored food and eggs.
